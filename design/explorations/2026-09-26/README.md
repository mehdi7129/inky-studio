# Inky Studio — exploration visuelle

Trois propositions de dashboard, données fictives, basées sur les fonctions de la version 0.3.3. Maquettes visuelles générées avec l’outil intégré imagegen. La direction bento blanc/noir a été retenue et intégrée dans `client/` ; les deux autres restent des explorations.

## Bento — blanc & noir

Fichier : `01-bento.png`

### Prompt

Use case: ui-mockup. Create a high-fidelity desktop web application UI design screenshot for Inky Studio, a self-hosted photo-frame control app. One full desktop dashboard, landscape 1600x1100, straight-on crisp flat interface, no browser chrome, no devices, no external presentation background or floating annotations. The entire image IS the usable app.
ART DIRECTION: premium restrained BENTO, BLACK AND WHITE FIRST. White canvas (#FAFAFA), white cards, near-black crisp typography and solid black primary buttons. Only tiny accents of cobalt, green and amber on small icons, state dots or count badges; absolutely no large pastel colored cards, no gradients, no dark theme. Beautiful asymmetric bento grid, generous negative space, subtle hairline gray borders, moderate 20px card corners, extremely subtle shadow, coherent simple line icons. Precise modern sans typography, refined 8px spacing rhythm, large readable functional labels. Calm and clean yet not empty or bland.
TOP NAV: Inky Studio wordmark with a simple outlined frame square mark at left. Compact navigation 'Tableau de bord' active, 'File d’attente', 'Historique', 'Paramètres'. Far right a small green dot and 'Cadre connecté'. Below nav a heading 'Votre cadre, à votre rythme.' and small supporting text 'Une nouvelle photo. Chaque jour.' Top-right solid black button '+ Ajouter une photo'.
BENTO COMPOSITION: Main large card left occupying two thirds of width, small top label 'Sur le cadre', then a beautifully composed landscape photograph of Mediterranean limestone cliffs and deep blue calm sea, subtle analog grain, warm white buildings distant. Display photograph in a full uncropped 5:3 ratio, fine interior frame. Under image a restrained footer: title 'Un matin à Cassis', label 'Affichée aujourd’hui à 08:00', outline 'Précédente' and black 'Afficher la suivante →' buttons. This card is visually dominant. Right third stacks a small schedule card with tiny amber clock icon, 'Prochain changement', 'Demain, 08:00', little 'Quotidien' pill and an understated 'Modifier' action; below it a medium white upload card with tiny cobalt plus icon, 'Une nouvelle vue ?', 'Glissez une photo ici', and secondary 'Choisir une photo'. In the lower row, a wide queue card titled 'À suivre' with a tiny count badge '4 photos', four landscape thumbnails (mountain lake, architectural shadows, coastal pine, warm desert), concise short captions and 'Voir la file →'. Small lower-right hardware card has an outlined frame icon, 'Inky Impression', '800 × 480 · 6 couleurs', compact green dot 'Prêt'. Avoid unnecessary device metrics. Everything fits comfortably within the screenshot without clipping and with readable controls.
PRODUCT CONSTRAINTS: This is an e-ink photo-frame app, not analytics or marketing. No charts, revenue, storage percentages, clocks in giant decorative type, cloud features, playback progress, avatars or fabricated analytics. No purple gradients or glass effects. Do not depict dither preview as the actual device display. Use correct French labels exactly where provided; text sharp and legible. Keep all UI almost entirely black/white with tiny functional colors. Layout should feel immediately buildable in React/CSS.

## Studio — anthracite & ambre

Fichier : `03-studio.png`

### Prompt

Use case: ui-mockup. Create a high-fidelity desktop web application UI design screenshot for Inky Studio, a self-hosted photo-frame control app. One full desktop dashboard, landscape 1600x1100, straight-on crisp flat interface, no browser chrome, no devices, no external presentation background or floating annotations. The entire image IS the usable app.
ART DIRECTION: DARK PRECISION PHOTO STUDIO. Charcoal background (#151617), slightly lifted panels (#1D1F20), warm off-white high-contrast text, muted gray secondary text still readable, a tightly controlled muted amber accent (#D7AB65) on one primary button and current selection details. Crisp technical sans-serif, small monospace only for resolution/time. Hairline dividers, modest 6–10px button rounding. No bento cards, no big rounded tiles, no gradients, neon or glow. Professional yet calm and simple like a purposeful photography workbench, not hacker/gaming software.
STRUCTURE: horizontal top bar with outline frame-square logo and 'Inky Studio' at left, navigation 'Tableau de bord' selected, 'Historique', 'Paramètres' toward center, connected status dot + 'Cadre connecté' right.
Below, a three-zone working layout filling available width with very careful spacing.
LEFT FILMSTRIP COLUMN about 250px wide: title 'File d’attente' and small '4' badge, full-width subtle outline '+ Ajouter une photo'. Ordered vertical queue items 01–04, each containing a generous photographic landscape thumbnail and short caption: 'Lac d’Annecy', 'Lignes d’ombre', 'Pin maritime', 'Dunes'. Active first item has a quiet amber hairline indicator. Include a tiny six-dot drag handle, no fake analytics.
CENTER MAIN CANVAS about 900px wide: large title 'Sur le cadre' and smaller status 'Affichée aujourd’hui à 08:00'. Inside an uncluttered dark workspace, a beautiful full 5:3 landscape photo of Mediterranean limestone cliffs and deep-blue calm sea with warm distant white buildings, subtle analogue grain. A slim black frame and ample dark mat help the photo stand out. Show entire photo with no extra crop overlays, no invented color preview. Under it photo title 'Un matin à Cassis' and filename 'cassis.png', subtle '← Précédente' button plus clear AMBER primary button 'Afficher la suivante →'. Do not include play/pause/seekbar.
RIGHT INSPECTOR COLUMN about 250px wide: aligned open sections separated by thin rules, not floating cards. 'Diffusion' with three-segment selector 'Quotidien' active / 'Intervalle' / 'Manuel', time field '08:00', note 'Prochain changement demain'. 'Rendu' with a simple 'Saturation' slider, value '1,00', hint 'Appliquée à la prochaine photo'. 'Votre cadre' with 'Inky Impression', '800 × 480', '6 couleurs', small green dot 'Prêt'. Navigation and captions readable with good contrast, no tiny gray text.
Everything fits in the screenshot with comfortable negative space and clean structural alignment. Distinct from the other concepts by true workbench architecture: vertical filmstrip / central canvas / inspector. Minimal, functional, high quality, immediately buildable. No account avatars, cloud, AI tools, social actions, charts, telemetry, revenue, storage meters or features beyond photo upload/queue/display/schedule/saturation. Accurate sharp French text, believable UX.
